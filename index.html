Research Projects

🦠 A Bacterial Pathway for Homotaurine Catabolism via Sulfopropanoate, University of Melbourne

The project aimed to find bacteria that can degrade homotaurine as a sulfur substrate. My co-supervisor (Dr Burchill) and I visited different ponds and soil sites near the University of Melbourne to collect random samples, hoping to isolate this target bacterium. After some time, I successfully isolated the bacteria, so after DNA extraction, we sent the results to proteomics colleagues to identify the species. Meanwhile, I performed enzyme kinetics analysis for the homotaurine substrate by optimising pH and buffer composition. I also monitored bacterial growth and performed sulfate and sulfite analyses.

🦠 Structural and kinetic insights into the stereospecific oxidation of R-2,3-dihydroxypropanesulfonate by DHPS-3-dehydrogenase from Cupriavidus pinatubonensis, University of Melbourne

The project aimed to find optimal conditions for high-rate enzymatic reaction. As mentioned above, I also performed enzyme kinetics, but this time for the DHPS substrate by optimising pH and buffer composition.

⚙Evaluation of low shear-force associated gravity-based pumping of fungal suspensions, AAU, VentriLabs

This project examined how different pump types affect shear forces on fungal suspensions. My group and I received a prototype from VentriLabs: a membrane-based, low-shear-force pump. Its effect on the fungal suspension was aimed to be lower than that of the peristaltic pump, which is considered a medium-to-high shear-force pump. Thus, image recognition was used to monitor pellet sizes over time, and a statistical test was used to assess deviations among the samples.

🛢 Side effects of formaldehyde releasers in petroleum refineries, AAU

This project focused on fouling formation in refineries where crude oil is delivered from upstream and formaldehyde-based scavengers are used. My group and I aimed to develop a reagent that could prevent this solid formation, which would ultimately reduce the cost of refineries' physical cleaning and maintenance. Moreover, the solid is toxic and hazardous to people and the environment, so our plan must reduce its environmental and health impact.
